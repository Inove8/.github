# Code of Conduct

Inove8 projects follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**In short:** be respectful, be constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated in any Inove8 space — issues, pull requests, discussions or chat.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, email **hello@inove8.com** with the subject line `Code of Conduct`. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't align with this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful.
