# Security Policy

We take the security of Inove8 software and our clients' systems seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security problems.**

- Email **hello@inove8.com** with the subject `SECURITY: <short summary>`, or
- Use GitHub's **"Report a vulnerability"** button (Security tab → Advisories) on the affected repository.

Please include the affected repository, steps to reproduce, potential impact and any proof of concept.

## What to expect

| Step | Target time |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement | within 2 business days |
| Initial assessment | within 5 business days |
| Fix or mitigation plan | depends on severity; we'll keep you updated |

We're happy to credit researchers who report responsibly once a fix is released.
