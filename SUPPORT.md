# Getting help

- 🐞 **Found a bug in one of our open repos?** Open an issue using the *Bug report* form.
- 💡 **Have an idea?** Open an issue using the *Feature request* form.
- 🔐 **Security issue?** See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) — please don't post it publicly.
- 💼 **Want to work with us on a project?** [Book a discovery call](https://www.inove8.com/contact?utm_source=github&utm_medium=support) or email **hello@inove8.com**. We reply within one business day.
