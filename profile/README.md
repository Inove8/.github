## 👋 About Inove8

**Inove8** is a full-service technology studio headquartered in **Noida, India**, working with clients across **India, the Middle East and North America**.

We started Inove8 to stop clients choosing between *good engineering* and *good marketing*. Too many businesses end up with a beautiful site that doesn't convert, or a campaign driving traffic to a broken funnel — so we own the whole outcome. Engineering, design, cloud, marketing and consulting sit in one team, so nothing gets lost between vendors.

> 🎯 **Mission** — Give ambitious teams one accountable partner for their entire digital roadmap, so strategy, build and growth never work against each other.
>
> 🔭 **Vision** — To be the first call for founders and enterprise teams across South & Southeast Asia whenever *"we think, we imagine"* needs to become something real.

## 🔄 How we work

```text
  Discovery  ──▶  Scoping workshop  ──▶  Fixed-price proposal  ──▶  Build  ──▶  Launch  ──▶  Hypercare  ──▶  Monthly support
                    (paid or free,           or monthly retainer                              (every build)     & iteration
                     depending on size)
```

- **Taking over a project?** We regularly onboard onto existing codebases and marketing accounts — starting with a short audit so we know what we're inheriting.
- **After launch?** Every build ships with a hypercare window; most clients move to a monthly support plan for updates, monitoring and iteration.
